# 🖤 DARK MINIMALIST Y2K THEME

## Overview
A clean, sophisticated dark theme that combines Y2K aesthetics with modern minimalism. Less "shiny metallic," more "underground club."

## Design Philosophy
- **Pure Black Base**: Deep #000000 backgrounds for maximum contrast
- **Strategic Purple Accents**: Subtle neon purple highlights that glow on interaction
- **Minimal Gradients**: Clean, simple transitions instead of heavy effects
- **Brutalist Meets Y2K**: Sharp lines with tasteful glow effects
- **Negative Space**: Let the products breathe

## Key Elements

### 1. Header
```css
- Background: rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.95) with strong blur
- Border: Thin purple line (rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.2))
- Hover: Subtle purple glow appears
- Clean, glass-morphism effect
```

### 2. Backgrounds
```css
- Homepage: Pure black with subtle radial purple gradients
- Sections: Solid black with minimal overlays
- Focus on content, not backgrounds
```

### 3. Product Cards
```css
- Base: Dark translucent (rgba(10, 10, 10, 0.8))
- Border: Thin purple outline (rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.15))
- Hover: Purple glow + subtle lift
- Purple gradient overlay on hover
```

### 4. Buttons
```css
Add to Cart:
- White background, black text
- Purple gradient sweep on hover
- Clean, high contrast

CTA Buttons:
- Transparent with purple border
- Purple tint background on hover
- Subtle glow effect
```

### 5. Color Palette
```css
Primary:
- #000000 (Pure Black)
- #0a0a0a (Almost Black)

Accents:
- #8b5cf6 (Purple)
- #a855f7 (Lighter Purple)

Interactive States:
- rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.1) - Subtle tint
- rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.3) - Medium glow
- rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.5) - Strong glow

Text:
- #ffffff (White)
- rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.7) - Muted white
```

### 6. Effects
- **Glow on Hover**: Purple neon glow (box-shadow)
- **Backdrop Blur**: Glass-morphism for header
- **Subtle Lifts**: Small translateY on hover
- **Purple Sweeps**: Gradient overlays on interaction

### 7. Typography
- Clean, bold sans-serif (Rajdhani)
- High contrast white on black
- Generous letter-spacing
- ALL CAPS for impact

## Removed Elements
- ❌ Metallic chrome effects
- ❌ Heavy shimmer animations
- ❌ Silver gradients
- ❌ Multiple shadow layers
- ❌ Complex backgrounds

## Added Elements
- ✅ Clean glass-morphism
- ✅ Strategic purple accents
- ✅ Minimal, tasteful glows
- ✅ Brutalist structure
- ✅ Negative space

## Vibe
- **Underground rave culture**
- **Early 2000s minimalism**
- **Cyberpunk without the cheese**
- **Premium streetwear**
- **Rebellious but refined**

## Performance
- Faster rendering (fewer gradients)
- Cleaner animations
- Better contrast/readability
- Modern and timeless

*This is a more sophisticated, less gimmicky approach that lets the products and brand speak for themselves.*
