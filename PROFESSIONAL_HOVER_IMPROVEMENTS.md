### **Multi-Layer Effects:**

#### **1. Card Elevation (Lift)**
- Lifts up **12px** (was 8px)

- Feels responsive and premium

#### **2. Shadow System**
- **Dual shadows** for depth:
  - Purple glow: `0 25px 50px rgba(139, 92, 246, 0.25)`

- Creates realistic 3D effect

#### **3. Border Highlight**
- Border changes to purple on hover
- Smooth color transition
- Clear visual feedback

#### **4. Gradient Overlay**
- Subtle purple gradient appears
- Fades in smoothly from 0% to 5% opacity
- Adds sophistication

#### **5. Image Effects**
- **Slower zoom:** 0.6s duration (more elegant)

- Smooth, buttery animation

#### **6. Price Tag Animation**
- **Scales up:** 1.05x
- **Slight rotation:** -2 degrees (playful detail)
- **Purple glow intensifies**
- Draws attention to pricing

#### **7. Product Name Highlight**
- Text color changes to purple
- Smooth color transition
- Reinforces brand identity

#### **8. Info Section Lift**
- Product info lifts up 2px
- Subtle but noticeable
- Everything moves together

#### **9. Overlay Effect**
- **Gradient overlay:** Black to purple
- Smooth fade-in (0.4s)
- Professional appearance

#### **10. "View Product" Button**
- **Slides up from bottom** (translateY animation)
